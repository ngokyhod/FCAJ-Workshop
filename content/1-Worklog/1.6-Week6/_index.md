*   Gain in-depth knowledge of Amazon Route 53 and cloud-based DNS management solutions.

*   Consolidate and systematize all AWS knowledge learned in the previous 05 weeks (IAM, VPC, EC2, RDS, S3, CloudFront).
*   Develop a plan to apply AWS infrastructure to the actual deployment architecture of the project.
